# Narrow Library Bedroom

Working drawing set for a small, windowless, narrow room that has to be a bedroom and a library at the same time.

**Room:** 13'-8" × 6'-4" × 9'-0". No window. Door on the near short wall, swinging out.

## The constraint everything follows from

A standard twin mattress is 75" long. The room is 76" wide. The bed spans wall to wall with **1" of total slack**, which rules out any conventional bed frame and forces a plinth built to the room. That plinth then gives back roughly 60 cu ft of drawer storage.

## The scheme

- Bed spanning the full width at the far end, on a built-in plinth with three drawers
- Stepped shelf run down the left wall: 18" closed base to 32" AFF, then 12" open book shelving to the ceiling, with a 6" ledge at the step
- Two shelves above the bed on the far wall, both clearing a 58" head-strike line
- Right wall left bare and wall-washed, which is what makes a 6'-4" room stop reading as a corridor

Depth sits low, below the sightline, so the room measures 58" clear at the floor but still reads 64" wide at eye level.

## Before building

Two things are flagged in the spec notes and neither is a design question:

- In much of the US a room without an egress window cannot legally be designated a bedroom. It does not change how the room works, but it can affect appraisal and resale.
- A sealed interior room used for sleeping needs real air exchange. Confirm supply and return.

Verify all dimensions on site before fabrication, particularly the 76" width at floor level. The 1" bed tolerance leaves no margin for out-of-square walls.
